Takeaway: the day-25 due date is pulling real weight even in April, its first month — 18.6% of April's collected cash landed exactly on day 25, alongside a big early-payer cluster (49.7% by day 19) and a lingering month-end habit (18.4% in day 28-31). May looks similar in shape (15.1% on day 25, 17.7% in 28-31), so the shift hasn't fully displaced old end-of-month rhythm yet for either month. June's bucket isn't meaningful yet — only 14.6% of June invoices are collected so far (3 payments), and all landed early, so its 100%-in-day-1-19 split will change a lot as more June invoices get paid. On-time rate (by due date) is still climbing toward the pre-change baseline: 45.4% for May and 48.1% for April vs 65.2% under the old end-of-month rule for March — but within +5 days of due date, April (73.0%) and May (69.3%) aren't too far apart.
