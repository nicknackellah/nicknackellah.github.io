Takeaway: the 25th is now the anchor. August is the clearest example — 62.1% of August's invoiced amount was paid by the Sep 25 due date and 76.1% within five days of it, both the best since the rule changed (by due date: April 48.0%, May 45.5%, June 42.7%, July 47.9%). Half of August's collected cash (50.7%) landed in the day 20-25 window. July shows the more typical shape once a month is fully collected: about half paid by day 19 (54.1%), a spike on day 25 (18.0%), and a smaller late tail in day 28-31 (14.6%). The +5-day rate is weaker for July (54.8%) because about 44% of July's invoiced amount was paid well after the due date. August's remaining 22 invoices (฿1.01M) include 6 exception brands due Oct 7–15.
